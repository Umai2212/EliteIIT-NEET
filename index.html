What is the NEET Exam?

NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test) is India's only national entrance exam for MBBS, BDS, and Ayush admissions, conducted by NTA. In 2024, over 24 lakh students competed for approximately 1,08,940 MBBS seats — making it one of the most competitive exams in the country.

Conducted byNational Testing Agency (NTA)
Exam modeOffline (pen and paper)
Duration3 hours 20 minutes
Total questions200 (attempt any 180)
Total marks720
SubjectsPhysics, Chemistry, Biology
Marking scheme+4 correct / −1 wrong
FrequencyOnce a year
